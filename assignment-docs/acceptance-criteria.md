Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance Criterion: The landing page displays my name and a concise professional role.
How I will verify it: Open the website and verify that my name and professional role appear correctly on the landing page.

Acceptance Criterion: The portfolio displays my professional experience using information from content-notes.md.
How I will verify it: Compare the Experience section of the website with content-notes.md to verify accuracy.

Acceptance Criterion: The portfolio displays my education and professional skills accurately.
How I will verify it: Compare the Education and Skills sections with content-notes.md to confirm that the information is correct.

Acceptance Criterion: The navigation links move to the correct sections of the portfolio.
How I will verify it: Click each navigation link and confirm that it opens or moves to the intended section.

Acceptance Criterion: The website remains readable and usable on a narrow mobile viewport.
How I will verify it: Resize the browser to a mobile-sized viewport and verify that the content remains readable and usable.
